# Questions for the made-up bakery history

Every question, answer and place here is invented, like `history.json`. Kinds: A a detail, B a decision and its reason, C something that changed over time, D work that was finished, E something left open and closed later, F a trap (the honest answer is that it is not in the history). "Where" names the conversation and the message sequence that hold the answer.

**1. A. Which flour supplier did we pick, and how much do we order?**
- Answer: Hartwell Mills; forty 12.5 kg bags, delivered the first Monday of each month.
- Where: conv-b02 seq 2-3

**2. B. Why did we stop buying flour from Dunmore Grain?**
- Answer: Dunmore raised the price of bread flour by 18 percent from February.
- Where: conv-b02 seq 0

**3. C. What is the free delivery threshold?**
- Answer: $60, from Monday March 9.
- Earlier state: $40 (set January 12).
- Where: conv-b06 seq 0-1

**4. C. What time do we open on Saturdays?**
- Answer: 7:30 am, from April 18.
- Earlier state: 8 am (set February 2).
- Where: conv-b09 seq 0-1

**5. D. What fixed the order form letting people order after the cutoff?**
- Answer: The form's timezone was set to Pacific/Auckland (the server clock was UTC); the cutoff is 2 pm.
- Where: conv-b04 seq 3

**6. A. When do we feed the sourdough starter, and what is it called?**
- Answer: 6 am and 6 pm; the starter is called Gerald.
- Where: conv-b05 seq 2-3

**7. E. Did we find someone for the Saturday counter?**
- Answer: Yes. Mei, from May 9, $27 an hour, 7:15 am to 3:15 pm.
- Earlier state: open, waiting on Tom's cousin (March 11); she said no.
- Where: conv-b11 seq 0-3

**8. A. What do we charge for hot cross buns?**
- Answer: $4.50 each, or six for $24.
- Where: conv-b08 seq 2-3

**9. B. Which newsletter tool did we choose, and when does the newsletter go out?**
- Answer: Postwing (it has scheduled sends); every Thursday at 4 pm.
- Where: conv-b10 seq 2-5

**10. D. Who repaired the deck oven, and what did it cost?**
- Answer: Kessler Service replaced the thermostat on May 19 for $380, with a 12 month warranty.
- Where: conv-b12 seq 2-5

**11. A. How long is the bulk ferment, and at what temperature?**
- Answer: Five hours at 24 degrees.
- Where: conv-b05 seq 3-5

**12. B. Why is there no chocolate hot cross bun?**
- Answer: To avoid a second allergen line during Easter week.
- Where: conv-b08 seq 2-3

**13. F. What did we decide about opening a second shop in Rosedale?**
- Honest answer: Nothing. A second shop, and Rosedale, are not in the history.

**14. F. Which accountant did we switch to after Bellamy?**
- Honest answer: No accountant is mentioned anywhere in the history.

**15. F. What price did we set for the Croissant Loaf?**
- Honest answer: A croissant loaf is not in the history.
