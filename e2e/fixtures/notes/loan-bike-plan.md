# Loan bike plan, June 2025

## The idea

Customers who commute by bike cannot be without one for three days while theirs is in the workshop. A loan bike keeps them riding and keeps them coming back to us. This note plans how many loan bikes we run and how the loan bikes are looked after.

## How many loan bikes

We start with 2 loan bikes. Two loan bikes is what we can store behind the counter without losing display space, and what we can afford to take out of the second-hand stock. The 2 loan bikes are a medium step-through and a large hybrid, which fits most of the people who ask.

## Rules for the loan bikes

A loan bike goes out only against a repair booked with us. The customer signs the loan sheet and leaves a card imprint. Loan bikes come back when the repair is collected. Each of the 2 loan bikes gets a safety check every time it returns: brakes, tyres, lights, quick releases.

## What the loan bikes cost us

Each loan bike is worth about $280 as second-hand stock. Running 2 loan bikes costs us roughly $40 a month in tubes, pads and time. If the 2 loan bikes are out more than half the time through the summer, we look at adding to them.
