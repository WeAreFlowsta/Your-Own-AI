# Warranty on repairs: draft policy, November 2024

## Where we are

We have never written down a warranty on repairs. When a customer comes back unhappy we fix it and say nothing more. That is generous and it is also unclear, and twice this autumn it led to an argument about a repair done five months before.

## The draft warranty on repairs

Our warranty on repairs is 30 days. If a repair we did fails within 30 days, we put it right at no charge for labour. Parts are covered by their maker's warranty, not ours. The 30-day warranty on repairs does not cover crash damage, wear items such as brake pads and tyres, or work the customer asked us to do against our advice.

## How the warranty on repairs is shown

The 30-day warranty on repairs is printed on every receipt and on a card at the counter. The job tag carries the collection date, which is the day the 30 days start.

## Open questions

Whether 30 days is long enough for wheel builds, where spokes settle over the first few hundred miles. Whether the warranty on repairs should be longer for customers on a service plan.
