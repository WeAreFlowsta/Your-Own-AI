# Workshop tools

## Calibration

The torque wrenches are calibrated every 6 months by Dunmore Instruments. The certificate goes in the blue folder. A torque wrench that has been dropped is taken out of use until it has been checked.

## The big items

- Truing stand: Kestrel T-40, bought second-hand in 2023.
- Compressor: 150 litre, in the yard shed. Drain the tank every Friday.
- Bearing press: shared with the frame builder next door.
- Two work stands with clamp heads; the left one is for e-bikes because it takes the weight.

## Lost tools

Every mechanic has a shadow board. A gap at closing time gets looked for before the lights go off.
