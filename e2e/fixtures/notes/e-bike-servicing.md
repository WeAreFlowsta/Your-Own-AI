# E-bike servicing

## What we can do

Battery and motor diagnostics with the Voltaire D2 tool. We can update firmware only for Brisa and Ondo motors; other makes go back to their dealer. A diagnostic costs the customer $45, taken off the bill if they go ahead with the repair.

## What we do not do

We do not open battery packs. A swollen or damaged battery goes in the sand bin in the yard and the fire service's non-emergency line is called.

## Booking

E-bikes go on the left work stand. Allow an extra half hour on any service for the software check.
