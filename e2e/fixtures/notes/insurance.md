# Insurance

Insurer: Marlow Mutual. The policy renews on March 14 each year.

- The excess on any claim is $750.
- Customers' bikes in our care are covered up to $6,000 each.
- Loan bikes are covered while out with a customer, provided the loan sheet is signed.
- Public liability covers the maintenance evenings and the spring ride.

Claims go through the broker, Hesketh and Lowe. Photograph everything before anything is moved.
