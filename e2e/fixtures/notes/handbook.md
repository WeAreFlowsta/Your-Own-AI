# Tern Street Cycles: shop handbook

The handbook is the current word on how the shop runs. Where an older note says something different, the handbook is right.

| What | Now |
|---|---|
| Opening hours | Tuesday to Saturday, 8:30 to 17:30 |
| Workshop labour rate | $95 an hour |
| Loan bikes | 4 |
| Warranty on repairs | 90 days |
| Card terminal | Pelham Pay |
| Deposit on special orders | 25 percent |

## Booking in a repair

Every bike is tagged at the counter with the customer's name and a job number. The mechanic who books it in writes the estimate on the tag. Anything over the estimate by more than $40 gets a phone call before the work goes on.

## Collecting

A bike not collected within 21 days gets a reminder text. Storage is charged from day 30.
